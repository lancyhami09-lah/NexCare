Why NexCare Makes a Difference

During urgent medical needs, going to the closest facility can lead to unexpected delays if it is completely filled or understaffed. NexCare evaluates five practical dimensions.

🛏️

Beds (30%)

Real-time ratio of available beds versus occupied beds.

📍

Distance (20%)

Calculated physical travel distance from your chosen locality.

👨‍⚕️

Doctors (20%)

Availability of on-duty physicians and specialists across departments.

🏥

Resources (15%)

Status of ICU beds, oxygen, lab, pharmacy, CT scan, and blood bank.

👥

Crowd Level (15%)

Current waiting queue and congestion to minimize wait times.
